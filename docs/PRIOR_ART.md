## Head-to-head pair

- **Same dataset:** Kubahoniyesu 2026. Falsifying result for R03 novelty: if the full text shows they already used the after-discharge items on facility births with discharge-known predictors, R03's Rwanda 2019-20 analysis is not new and only the 2025 temporal test would remain.
- **Same question elsewhere:** Pillay 2026. R03 cannot claim better outcome measurement than a prospective cohort; its possible contribution is representativeness, two survey waves and an outreach-capacity evaluation.

## What would be new (if Gate 1 passes) and what would not

- New: a facility-birth, post-discharge endpoint from the separate DHS items; discharge-time predictor restriction enforced in code; temporal validation 2019-20 → 2025; capture of missed dyads at a fixed outreach capacity vs simple rules.
- Not new: using DHS PNC data; using ML (RSF, GBST, RF, ANN already applied); associations with wealth, education, insurance.
