# Limitations (known before data)

- **Recall and reporting.** Checks are mother-reported up to two years after birth; "after discharge" checks may be misclassified, and don't-know responses are common for newborn checks in many surveys. Missed visits cannot be separated from reporting error without external data.
- **Timing reference.** If post-discharge timing is recorded from birth, a window mixes facility stay with time at home.
- **Survivor selection.** Requiring the newborn alive at window end conditions on a post-discharge outcome.
- **Discharge-time information.** DHS interviews happen months after birth; any predictor reconstructed from the interview carries recall error, and some characteristics (wealth, residence, marital status) are measured at interview, not discharge.
- **Not a facility register.** Survey dyads represent the population, not a specific facility's discharges; transport of a model to a facility setting needs separate validation.
- **Comparability.** Questionnaire phases differ across Rwanda 2019–20, Rwanda 2025, Kenya 2022 and Ethiopia 2024–25; geographic validation only where items match.
- **No causal interpretation** of predictors or of outreach.
